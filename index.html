About Me

Hello and welcome to my little corner of the art world!

I'm ANNA, a graphic designer and illustrator with 4 years of experience in bringing creative ideas to life. I specialize in creating personalized artwork and designs that reflect each client's unique vision, personality, and creativity.

My services include Live2D models and rigging, character design, profile pictures (PFPs), emotes, banners, stream assets, reference sheets, 3D models, VRChat assets, custom outfits, and other commission work.

I believe every character has a story worth telling, and I love helping creators bring their ideas to life through thoughtful designs and attention to detail. Whether you have a fully developed concept or just a small idea, I'm happy to work with you to turn your vision into something special.

As an artist, I value creativity, clear communication, and making sure my clients feel heard and comfortable throughout the commission process. Every project is an opportunity to create something meaningful, and I approach each commission with care, dedication, and a commitment to delivering artwork that feels personal to you.

Thank you for visiting ANNA and considering my work for your creative journey. I truly appreciate every person who supports my art, and I look forward to bringing your ideas to life!
