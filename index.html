About

The artist behind the work.

I design the visual identity of online creators, from the first sketch of a character to the last light in a 3D room.

My work sits where graphic design meets character art. I build VTuber models, logos, banners, emotes, illustrated scenes and 3D art rooms, and I keep them consistent, so a viewer recognises your channel from a single thumbnail.

I care about clean shapes, considered colour and detail that survives at small sizes. Every project is shaped around your personality and your audience, never around a template.
